## Lessons
1. Insider-fraud / "rigged" / secret hooks on huge brands (McDonald's, Disney) lead: 1.4-1.7K views at day 1-3. [signal, n=2]
2. "Free giveaway that cost a giant" stories (Hoover, Wrigley) land in the middle: ~1.1-1.4K.
3. Small/obscure names and "ordinary person" angles (Pudding man, Piggly Wiggly) underperform us: 390-720. Brand size matters more than the underdog angle for us right now.
4. Newest Shorts (Costco, Taco) started stronger than older ones at the same age, and like rates are 5-6%: the channel is warming up, or the 2-a-day / evening slots are helping. Too early to say.
5. The only long video got 1 view in 3.5 days. Owner paused long-form on 23 Nov: all effort goes to Shorts.

## Changes for next week
- Pick famous consumer brands with a hidden trick, scam or surprising reason (not obscure names).
- Keep 45-57 s; the best performers were 48-57 s.
- Two uploads per day now: keep the buffer at 4 ready scripts.
