# 017 — The Pringles Inventor Was Buried in a Pringles Can

hookTitle: ["BURIED IN A","PRINGLES CAN?!"]. Source: calendar item Wed 25 Nov. Famous-brand hidden-trick hook per playbook.
Title note: calendar draft said "Inventor"; sources say Baur DESIGNED the packaging (not the chip), so the title says "Designer".
