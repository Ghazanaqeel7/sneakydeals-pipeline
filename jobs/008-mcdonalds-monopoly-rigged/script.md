# 008 — McDonald's Monopoly Was Rigged by Its Own Security Chief

hookTitle: ["MONOPOLY","WAS RIGGED?!"]. Calendar brief: Fri 20 Nov item (picked early; the Popeyes item's key claim could not be confirmed, Coca-Cola could not be sourced).
