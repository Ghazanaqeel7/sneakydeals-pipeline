## Left out on purpose
Her name (private person). The year the guarantee began (1984 per AP/Deseret, 1979 per Snopes: conflict). Snopes-only $15M settlement figure and AP-only $150,000 driver settlement (single source, unconfirmed). The "driver killed a child" rumour (false per Snopes). Exact verdict date (Friday, inferred). Claim that the crash was caused by the guarantee (that was her argument, not a finding).

## Wording to watch
"She said" / "Domino's said": it is her claim and their denial, not a finding. Say "settled for far less", no figure. Dramatised illustration; driver and woman shown neutrally, no villain.
