# 018 McDonald's Secretly Collects Rent

## Shots
| hook | McDonald's secretly collects rent from its own franchisees. |
| s02 | Today, about a third of its revenue comes from rent. |
| s03 | It started with an executive named Harry Sonneborn, who worked with Ray Kroc. |
| s04 | His idea was simple. Do not only sell franchises. Own the land under them. |
| s05 | So McDonald's created a real estate company. It bought or leased land for new restaurants. |
| s06 | Then it rented that land to the franchisees. |
| s07 | Franchisees pay McDonald's a royalty on their sales. And they pay rent on top. |
| s08 | Rent is the bigger part, almost two thirds of what franchisees pay. |
| s09 | Today, more than nine in ten McDonald's restaurants are run by franchisees. |
| s10 | And McDonald's owns the land under roughly half of all its restaurants. |
| s11 | So a franchisee does not just sell burgers. They also pay McDonald's for the ground. |
| s12 | Franchisees sell the burgers. McDonald's collects the rent. |
