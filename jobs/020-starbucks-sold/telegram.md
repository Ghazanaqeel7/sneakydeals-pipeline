020 — Starbucks Was Sold for $3.8 Million (Six Stores)

Note: Gemini's draft said the founders kept some Seattle stores. That does not check out, so I left it out. Also left out: Schultz's exact leaving year (sources disagree).

Hook: "Starbucks once cost just three point eight million dollars."

Narration (11 shots, ~150 words, ~55s):
1. Starbucks once cost just three point eight million dollars.
2. That was the whole company. Six stores.
3. You see, Howard Schultz had worked at Starbucks. Then he left to start his own coffee bar.
4. Years later, one of the founders offered Starbucks to him first.
5. The price was three point eight million dollars. He had to find it fast.
6. He went to investors, and half the money came slowly.
7. Bill Gates Senior, the father of the Microsoft founder, helped him with the deal.
8. By August nineteen eighty-seven, Schultz had raised every dollar.
9. He bought the name, the roasting plant, and all six Seattle stores.
10. And the little Seattle chain was finally his to grow.
11. Six stores became a global chain. All from one three point eight million dollar check.
