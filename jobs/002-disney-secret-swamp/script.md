## Voice-over

Disney bought a secret swamp using fake names. You see, Walt Disney wanted to build a huge new park in Florida. But if sellers heard the name Disney, every acre would cost a fortune. So his team bought land through companies with names like Reedy Creek Ranch Corporation. One company was even called M. T. Lott. Say it fast. Swamp, scrub and cattle pasture went for less than two hundred dollars an acre. Starting in nineteen sixty-four, parcel by parcel, they gathered more than twenty-seven thousand acres. You see, that's about twice the size of Manhattan. Then a reporter, Emily Bavar, asked Walt about the mystery buyer on a press trip in California. In October nineteen sixty-five, her newspaper put it on the front page: the mystery buyer is Disney. So weeks later, Walt, his brother Roy and Florida's governor announced it in Orlando. Walt died in nineteen sixty-six. He never saw that swamp become Walt Disney World.

## Left out on purpose

- **"First acre $80, last acre $80,000"**: repeated by fan sites only, no primary source; sounds like a myth.
- **Exact total price** ("about $5 million"): not confirmed by two sources in this run.
- **"Prices jumped 1000%" / "$131,000 an acre"** as on-screen numbers: each figure is single-source, so the narration only says "would cost a fortune".
- **Walt's reaction to Bavar's question** ("bucket of water in his face"): that is Bavar's later recollection, and accounts differ on what Walt answered. No quote from a real person.
- **Lawyers' names** (Paul Helliwell, Robert Foster) and other shell names (Ayefour, Compass East, etc.): not needed and not double-sourced here.
- **The 1963 flyover and Reedy Creek Improvement District politics**: off-story, and the special district is a current political topic.

## Wording to watch

- "M. T. Lott": the voice must say "em tee lott". If Kokoro reads it oddly at build time, the SHOTS text may need "Em Tee Lott" (caption) while the on-screen sign keeps "M.T. LOTT".
- Say "secret" and "fake names", not "illegal" or "scam": using shell companies to buy land is legal.
- The front-page headline on screen is generic wording, not the real 1965 headline or masthead.
- Do not draw Cinderella Castle or any Disney logo/character; the castle at the end is a generic silhouette.
