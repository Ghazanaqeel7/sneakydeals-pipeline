s07 | The bet was simple. One free taste, and some people would come back and pay.
s08 | Roughly eight and a half million free drinks were given away this way.
s09 | By eighteen ninety-five, Coke was being served in every state.
s10 | The free glass was never a gift. It was advertising you could taste.
s11 | And Coca-Cola is widely considered the first company to ever offer coupons.
s12 | The first coupon in history was a free Coke.
