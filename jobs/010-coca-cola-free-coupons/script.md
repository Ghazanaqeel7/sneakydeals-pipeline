# 010 — Coca-Cola's Free Drink Tickets

hookTitle: ["FREE COKE","ON PURPOSE?!"]. Calendar brief: Mon 16 Nov item (first planned Short). Gemini draft used only as a start; its "1 in 10 Americans", "85% redeemed", "1894 start" and "mailed to millions" were NOT confirmed and are dropped.

|---|---|

## Fact map (2 sources each)
- Candler's free-drink tickets, 1887: History.com (Candler's idea, 1887), PYMNTS (1887, tickets for a free glass), Stacker/CouponBirds (1887).
- Tickets spread via magazines and direct mail: History.com (quotes Alexander Ketter), PYMNTS (mailed to neighbours' addresses).
- ~8.5 million free drinks: Stacker/CouponBirds, PYMNTS (also Mashed citing Wired). Timing differs (by 1913 vs "first few years"), so the script gives no date.
- Sold/served in every state by 1895: History.com, Stacker.
- "Widely considered the first company to offer coupons": History.com, Mashed/Wired ("believed first-ever coupon").

## Left out
1894 start, 1-in-10 Americans, 85% redemption (Gemini only, unconfirmed); pharmacist address-for-syrup deal (single source); who first thought of the ticket (Mashed credits Pemberton's bookkeeper; History, PYMNTS, Stacker credit Candler). Script credits Candler with the idea per 3 of 4 sources; owner may soften.

## Wording to watch
Say "drinks" not "coupons" for the 8.5 million (PYMNTS counts drinks). Dramatised: tickets, fountain scenes are illustrations.
